# Memoir Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 30, 2026_

Memoir is an open-source personal archive app. This policy explains what it does with your data.

## Data stored on your device

Everything you save in Memoir (text, links, photos, videos and files) is stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. Memoir has no server and the developer never receives your data.

## Google Drive backup (optional)

If you choose **Continue with Google**, Memoir asks for the `drive.file` permission. With it, Memoir can only see and manage the files **it created** in your Google Drive (under a `Memoir` folder). It cannot read any other file in your Drive.

Backups are sent directly from your phone to your own Google Drive. You can disconnect at any time from the app, and revoke access at <https://myaccount.google.com/permissions>.

Memoir's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

## App lock

App lock uses your phone's own screen lock (PIN, pattern, password, fingerprint or face). Memoir never sees or stores your PIN or biometric data; the operating system handles them.

## Photos

Memoir only accesses photos you pick, and saves a photo to your gallery only when you tap **Save**.

## Update check

Once a day, the Android app asks GitHub for the latest Memoir release to tell you when a new version is available. The request contains no personal data, and you choose whether to download the update.

## No tracking

Memoir contains no ads, analytics or tracking.

## Contact

Questions? Open an issue in this repository.
